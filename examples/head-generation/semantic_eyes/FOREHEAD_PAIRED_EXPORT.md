# Paired export admission and optional continuous guidance

Shared fitting always checks the actual float32 neutral and MouthOpen polygons using `source_polygon_validity.polygon_diagonals`: at input, before selecting a feasible candidate, and after final scalar reconstruction. The candidate field is the complete saved current field with sampled variable fields replaced; final admission uses reconstructed `new_delta`. Region1/2 faces are derived from immutable original-owner region inheritance, including untouched skin faces. Invalid checkpoints fail before optimization. Unsupported skin polygons fail closed rather than receiving an unverified pass.

`paired_diagonal_guidance=False` preserves the former optimization-row layout (an empty named block) while retaining this mandatory export gate. Explicit `True` additionally chooses one initially eligible common diagonal and a dominant projection axis/sign separately for neutral and MouthOpen. Local continuous rows retain positive edges/areas, projected triangle winding, triangle-normal/area alignment and coherence, cross-pose area alignment greater than .1, and the chosen dominant-axis branch. Native scale-dependent area/edge tolerances are unchanged. The continuous MouthOpen surrogate includes a fixed per-vertex offset equal to `float32(p0+d0) - double(p0+d0)`, computed at the actual saved input. This aligns its initial projection branch with the exporter. The offset is never applied to output positions, fields, source parameters, or frozen references; it remains constant throughout the fit. Fixed positive scaling and a small accepted-start-derived working floor strengthen, never relax, final acceptance.

This is a **conservative per-fit parameterization**, not a permanent anatomical constraint or an equivalent description of all valid meshes. Valid changes of diagonal or projection axis may be blocked. Exact axis ties, unsupported movable triangles/ngons, or an initially nonpositive continuous reference fail closed; they do not prove global infeasibility. A future explicit branch-change comparison may be needed. The authoritative float32 check remains necessary even when every continuous row passes.

All rows depend only on their quad's variables, including the sampled MouthOpen field. No dense finite-difference coloring dependency is introduced. The validated private comparison retained70 colors and admitted the same .0125 gap reduction in15 LP iterations /61.41 seconds; postgate-only attempts could not achieve that target in the bounded comparison. This is component evidence, not a general performance guarantee.

No original quality, frozen-field, pin, lower-turn, physical/front, whole-station/length, transverse-gap, or retained fairing guard is removed. Paired endpoint validity is not the independent dense bidirectional1mm check,729-state orientation test, native transport/readback, or visual acceptance.
